Family Fun Festival

A weekend of joy, laughter, and unforgettable family memories

📅 Saturday & Sunday, May 23-28 🕐 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM 📍 Spruce Street Harbor Park 💵 Free Entry

About This Festival

Join us for an incredible weekend celebration at the beautiful Delaware River waterfront! The Family Fun Festival at Spruce Street Harbor Park offers an oasis of entertainment, relaxation, and family bonding like no other.

Since 2014, this beloved warm-weather destination has been bringing families together with colorful hammocks, impressive programming, tasty snacks, and cold sips, all while enjoying stunning riverfront views.

Lounge in our iconic Grove with hundreds of multi-colored LED lights strung among the tree canopy, creating a magical atmosphere from dawn to dusk.

What to Expect

  • ✨ Floating gardens & colorful hammocks
  • 🎵 Live music and DJ performances
  • 🍔 Delicious food trucks and local vendors
  • 🎨 Interactive games and activities for all ages
  • 🌊 Beautiful riverfront views
  • 💡 Stunning LED light displays at night

Festival Highlights & Events

Experience these amazing activities throughout the festival weekend

Thursday Nights
🎸

Live & Local Concert Series

📅 Select Thursdays • 🕐 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Philadelphia area bands and DJs set the scene in the park during this free, all-ages evening concert series. Discover incredible local talent while enjoying the waterfront atmosphere. Bring your blanket, grab a drink, and let the music move you!

🎵 Live Music 🎉 All Ages 💫 Free
Thursday Dance
💃

Spruce Street Dance Nights

📅 Every Thursday • 🕐 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Tonight, we dance! Learn from the pros and show off your moves at this brand-new weekly dance social. Themes include country line dancing in June, swing dancing in July, and salsa in September. No experience necessary—just bring your energy!

🤠 Country Line 🎺 Swing 💃 Salsa
Friday Party
🎧

After Work Sessions

📅 Every Friday • 🕐 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM

The perfect way to kiss the workweek goodbye! This free weekly dance party at the Blue Anchor Barge—hosted by Shekhinah B.—features live DJ sets, special performances by surprise guests, and fun giveaways. Come unwind and celebrate the weekend!

🎧 DJ Sets 🎁 Giveaways 🍹 Bar
Saturday Energy
🎶

Cosmic Rhythms

📅 Select Saturdays • 🕐 8:00 PM - 11:00 PM

Music lovers unite! This free high-energy party hits the Lazy Hammock with a lineup of top-tier DJs spinning beats on old-school vinyl each month. Experience the warm, authentic sound of vinyl under the stars with an amazing crowd.

📀 Vinyl DJ 🌟 High Energy 🎵 Dance
Saturday Chill
🌅

Sunsets at Spruce

📅 Select Saturdays • 🕐 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Enjoy cool breezes and chill vibes on the water with all-ages live entertainment by HHH Radio, showcasing local artists and performers. Watch the sun set over the Delaware River while enjoying incredible live music in a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere.

🌅 Sunset Views 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Friendly 🎤 Local Artists
Monthly Concert
🎤

Saturday Summer Concert Series

📅 Last Saturday of Month • 🕐 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM

This vibrant music bash caps off each month with a free concert showcasing a lineup of new and established bands from Philly and beyond. Don't miss these epic performances that bring the community together for unforgettable musical moments!

🎸 Live Bands 🎊 Big Event 🆓 Free

Venue & Location

Spruce Street Harbor Park

📍 301 S. Columbus Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19106

A Delaware River waterfront oasis serving as Philadelphia's premier warm-weather destination since 2014. The park features floating gardens, colorful LED lights, and cozy open-air seating with stunning riverfront views.

Venue Highlights:

  • 🌳 The Grove - Iconic colorful hammocks (first-come, first-served)
  • 🎭 The Lazy Hammock - Performance venue with 16-foot video wall
  • 🍺 The Blue Anchor Barge - Waterfront bar with craft beers & cocktails
  • 🏪 The Trading Post - Bodega with local ice cream, snacks & merch

Getting There:

  • 🚇 SEPTA: Take the L line to 2nd Street or buses to Penn's Landing
  • 🚌 PHLASH: Hop off at Penn's Landing & Independence Seaport Museum stop
  • 🚲 Bike: Indego bike share stations available nearby
  • 🚗 Parking: DRWC lot at Market & Lombard, plus nearby lots
  • 🚶 Walking: Columbus Blvd at Spruce, Market or South streets
