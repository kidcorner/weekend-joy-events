Children's Art Workshop

Where creativity blooms and young artists shine

📅 Every Sunday 🕐 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM 📍 Spruce Street Harbor Park 💵 Free • All Supplies Included

About This Workshop

Let your children's imagination run wild at our weekly Family Funday with PopUpPlay! Newly revamped for 2025, this series of family-friendly Sunday afternoons features free make-and-take activities designed to inspire creativity and hands-on learning.

Every week brings new artistic adventures—from boat building and puppet making to lawn games and DIY catapults. All materials are provided, and activities are designed for children of all skill levels and ages.

Watch your kids' faces light up as they create, build, and play in a supportive environment surrounded by the beauty of the Delaware River waterfront.

What's Included

  • 🎨 All art supplies and materials provided
  • 👨‍🏫 Guided instruction from experienced facilitators
  • 🏠 Take-home creations every week
  • 🎮 Interactive lawn games and activities
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family-friendly environment
  • 🆓 Completely free (first-come, first-served)

👶 Perfect for ages 3-12

Activities are designed to engage children of all ages, with options for both beginners and more experienced young artists.

Weekly Art Activities

A different creative adventure every Sunday afternoon!

Build & Float
⛵

Boat Building Workshop

🗓️ First Sunday of Month • Ages 5+

Design and construct your own floating vessel using recycled materials, cork, and colorful sails. Test your creation in our water station and see which boat sails the smoothest. Take home your masterpiece and the engineering skills you learned!

⚙️ Engineering ♻️ Recycled Materials 💧 Water Play
Theater Fun
🎭

Puppet Making & Theater

🗓️ Second Sunday • Ages 3+

Create your own sock puppet character with googly eyes, yarn hair, and colorful felt accessories. Then bring your puppet to life in our mini puppet theater! Kids love creating stories and putting on shows for family and friends.

🧦 Sock Puppets 🎪 Mini Shows 📖 Storytelling
Outdoor Play
🎯

DIY Lawn Games

🗓️ Third Sunday • All Ages

Build your own set of lawn games! From ring toss to bean bag games, kids will decorate and customize their own game sets to take home. Then play together on our lawn area and enjoy friendly family competition in the sunshine!

🎲 Game Making 🎨 Customizing 🏆 Family Fun
Science & Art
🚀

DIY Catapults

🗓️ Fourth Sunday • Ages 6+

Combine physics and creativity! Build your own mini catapult using popsicle sticks, rubber bands, and a plastic spoon. Decorate it with markers and stickers, then test it out in our launching zone. How far can you make your pompom fly?

🔬 STEM Learning 🎯 Target Practice 💥 Physics Fun
Nature Art
🌺

Nature Collage & Painting

🗓️ Rotating Activity • Ages 4+

Explore the natural beauty of the waterfront! Collect leaves, flowers, and interesting textures to create beautiful collages or use as stamps for nature printing. Mix colors, press flowers, and make art inspired by the world around us.

🍃 Nature Inspired 🖌️ Painting 🌿 Eco-Friendly
Craft Special
✂️

Seasonal Craft Projects

🗓️ Rotating Monthly • Ages 3+

Celebrate the seasons with themed craft projects! From summer sun catchers to autumn leaf wreaths, each month brings special activities that connect kids to the changing seasons. All supplies provided, and projects are designed to be completed in one session.

🎃 Seasonal 🎁 Take Home ✨ Special Themes
📋

How It Works

  • Show Up: Activities run 1-4 PM every Sunday
  • First-Come, First-Served: Available while supplies last
  • No Registration: Just arrive and join the fun!
  • Free Supplies: Everything you need is provided
  • Take It Home: All projects are yours to keep
💡

Pro Tips

  • Arrive Early: Popular activities fill up quickly
  • Dress for Mess: Wear clothes that can get dirty
  • Bring Water: Stay hydrated during outdoor activities
  • Stay & Play: Enjoy the hammocks and lawn after crafting
  • Check Schedule: Activities change weekly
🎒

What to Bring

  • Enthusiasm: A creative spirit!
  • Sun Protection: Hat and sunscreen for outdoor activities
  • Camera: Capture those creative moments
  • Bag: To carry home finished projects
  • Snacks: Or grab food from our vendors!

Location & Facilities

Spruce Street Harbor Park

📍 301 S. Columbus Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19106

Our workshops take place in the beautiful setting of Spruce Street Harbor Park on the Delaware River waterfront. After crafting, families can enjoy the park's many amenities!

Park Amenities:

  • 🎨 Dedicated craft station area
  • 🧺 Shaded seating and hammocks
  • 🚻 Clean restroom facilities
  • 🍦 The Trading Post for snacks and drinks
  • 🎮 Additional play areas and lawn games
  • ♿ Accessible facilities and pathways

Getting There:

The park is easily accessible via public transportation, bike, or car. See the Family Fun Festival page for detailed directions!
